# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Taiwan-based marketing teams, SEO professionals, digital agencies, and brand managers who want their brand to appear in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity answers. They understand digital marketing but may be new to GEO. They evaluate tools seriously and distrust overpromising.

## Product Purpose

Wazcher is the authorized Citora reseller in Taiwan. Citora is the world's first closed-loop Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) platform — it monitors, analyzes, optimizes, A/B tests, and deploys AI-readable brand profiles so brands get cited by AI search engines. Wazcher adds local Chinese support, dedicated account management, and Taiwan market expertise on top of Citora's platform.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, sharp, grounded. Not flashy — confident. The product does serious work; the brand should feel like it knows what it's doing without needing to shout about it.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI startup landing pages (dark bg + purple gradient + "supercharge your workflow")
- Overly corporate consulting sites (cold, grey, stock photography)
- Sites that look like every other GEO tool (dashboard screenshots + generic feature grids)

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't claim.** Use real metrics, real comparisons, real copy — not buzzwords.
2. **Earn trust through specificity.** "Chi-squared significance, Cohen's h effect size" beats "AI-powered insights."
3. **Local first.** The design should feel at home in Taiwan's digital landscape, not like a translated US SaaS template.
4. **Restraint signals confidence.** One strong accent color. One clear hierarchy. Whitespace that breathes.
5. **Practice what we preach.** A GEO platform should itself be structured and AI-readable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. All text at sufficient contrast. Keyboard navigable.
