The Problem

AI Search Has Changed Everything.

Users no longer scroll ten blue links. They ask AI directly, and AI picks winners. Is your brand one of them?

"Your brand ranks #1 on Google, but ChatGPT has never heard of you."

Traditional SEO optimizes for link-based algorithms. AI engines work differently: they synthesize answers from training data, RAG retrieval, and real-time web access. A top Google ranking does not guarantee AI visibility.

⚠️

Invisible to AI Search

Brands that AI cannot understand, find, or verify simply do not get cited, regardless of their Google ranking.

📉

Competitors Get the Credit

When AI recommends your category but not your brand, customers go to competitors. The gap widens every day.
